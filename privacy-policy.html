Privacy Policy

ECO Delivery Partner

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ECO Grocery ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when delivery partners use the ECO Delivery Partner mobile application ("App"). Please read it before using the App.

1. Information the App may handle

  • Account and contact information: information used to create or manage a delivery-partner account, such as your name, phone number, and email address, where those details are requested.
  • Delivery information: assigned orders, delivery addresses, order status, delivery notes, and related delivery activity.
  • Location information: if you grant location permission and use a location or tracking feature, the App may access your device location to support delivery assignment, navigation, and delivery tracking. Background location, if requested by a version of the App, is used only for the stated delivery-tracking purpose while that feature is active.
  • Technical information: information such as device and app version, diagnostic records, and logs may be processed to maintain security and troubleshoot problems.

The exact information handled depends on the features enabled in the version of the App you use and the permissions you grant.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to authenticate delivery partners, display and manage assigned orders, coordinate and track deliveries, provide customer or operational support, maintain App security, troubleshoot errors, and improve the service.

3. Location permission and tracking

Location is used only when the relevant location feature is enabled and you grant the required device permission. You can manage or revoke location permission in your Android device settings. If you turn it off, features that depend on location, such as live delivery tracking or location-based navigation, may not work.

4. When information may be shared

Information may be made available to authorised ECO Grocery personnel who manage orders and deliveries; to a customer where necessary to coordinate or track that customer's delivery; and to service providers that support functions such as app hosting, authentication, databases, maps, or diagnostics. Service providers may process information only as needed to provide their services. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion requests

Information is kept for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the delivery service, meet legitimate business needs, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable requirements. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact us at the email below. We may need to retain some records where required for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for delivery partners and is not designed for children. Children should not create or use a delivery-partner account.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or our data practices change. The latest version will be published at this webpage, with its updated effective date.
